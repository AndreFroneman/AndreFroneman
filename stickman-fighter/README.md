# Margin Brawl

A stickman fighting game drawn on notebook paper. Ballpoint (blue) and Red Pen (red) fight on the ruled lines of a homework page, best of three rounds.

It is one self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no dependencies. Open it in a browser to play. It also works on GitHub Pages or any static host.

## Modes

- **1 player vs CPU**: Easy, Normal or Hard.
- **2 players, one keyboard**: both players share one keyboard.
- On phones and tablets, an on-screen stick and buttons appear for player 1.

## Controls

| | Ballpoint (P1) | Red Pen (P2) |
|---|---|---|
| Move / jump / crouch | `W` `A` `S` `D` | Arrow keys |
| Punch | `F` | `,` or Numpad 1 |
| Kick | `G` | `.` or Numpad 2 |
| Ink blot | `H` | `/` or Numpad 3 |
| Block | `R` | `L` or Numpad 0 |

When playing solo, either key set controls Ballpoint. `Esc` pauses and `M` mutes the sound.

## Moves

- **Punch chain**: press punch up to three times for jab, jab, cross. If a hit connects, a kick or ink blot can follow it.
- **Uppercut**: down + punch. It launches the opponent into the air and beats jump-ins.
- **Sweep**: down + kick. It hits low and knocks the opponent down. Crouch-block to stop it.
- **Air attacks**: punch or kick while jumping. These hit overhead, so the defender has to stand to block them.
- **Dash**: tap forward twice. Tap back twice for a backdash, which is briefly invulnerable.
- **Ink blot**: a projectile that costs a quarter of the ink meter.
- **Scribble Fury**: down + ink when the meter is full. It is a dashing super with seven hits that ends in a launcher.

The ink meter fills when you land hits, take hits or block.
